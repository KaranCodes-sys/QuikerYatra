# Roadmap

## Day 1: Foundation
Architecture, domain types, design tokens, SVG system, component primitives, API abstractions, mock boundaries, documentation and quality gates.

## Phase 2: Customer MVP
OTP auth, Home, MOVE bike rides, SEND parcel delivery, estimates, booking, tracking, PINs, payment handoff, ratings and history.

## Phase 3: Rider MVP
Rider registration, KYC, vehicle, online/offline, unified job queue, ride and delivery execution, earnings and support.

## Phase 4: Business MVP
Business registration, create delivery, tracking, delivery history, proof and basic invoices.

## Phase 5: Admin MVP
Operations dashboards for users, riders, businesses, rides, deliveries, KYC, pricing, payments, support and analytics.

## Post-MVP
Scheduled deliveries, hotel mode, bulk delivery, staff roles, multi-stop, City Pass, route optimization, bundling, business API, local commerce and multi-city expansion.
